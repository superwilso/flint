Every job below uses these, and they are remembered.

Copy the library to the player

Show what would happen first: it lists what would be copied and removed, and changes nothing. Copy then does exactly that. Flint writes into the MUSIC folder of the player and the card.

One song on two albums

The same recording on an album and a best-of, or a single and its album. Stored once, a sync copies one file and Cinder still lists the song under both albums. Look lists the pairs with numbers; fill in your answers and look again to save them.

Back up the player's data

The playlists, likes, ratings, play counts, settings, palettes and play log made on the player, into Flint's data folder. Not the music.

Put a backup back

Copies the player as it is first, so a restore can be undone too.

Play counts

Tracks the player never counted get their plays from its scrobble log, and counts follow albums that moved between the player and the card. A count the player has is never changed.

Playlists made on the player

Read the library

Reads every track once and remembers it, so the rest is quick. Run it after adding music.

Is it really lossless?

Looks for FLAC files made from MP3s or other lossy files, and fake high resolution.

Tags

Albums that would show split on the player, tracks with no number, and files with no artist or title. Changes nothing.

Lyrics

Fetches synced lyrics from LRCLIB for tracks that have none, and saves each as a .lrc beside the track in your library, never replacing one. The next sync takes them to the player.

Send plays to Last.fm

Reads the player's scrobble log; what Last.fm accepts is then removed from it. Sign in under Settings first.

Likes and loved tracks

Last.fm

Needs an API key of your own: create one at Last.fm. The password is sent to Last.fm once and never stored.

Music Center

Reads the SensMe analysis Music Center already did (it changes nothing there), so Flint does not need to run it again.
